Find your sun

Sun tracker calculates the sun's exact position in the sky for where you are right now, giving you accurate sunrise, sunset, daylight hours, and position information.

Your location is only used for this calculation. It never leaves your device and no server ever sees it. If you ask us to remember your choice, only that preference is saved on this device — your location is still fetched fresh each visit, never stored. If you decline, the app uses Greenwich, London instead.
